Dearest Morium Tabassum,

From the very moment you walked into my life, every ordinary second transformed into something extraordinary—something deeply worth holding onto. In a world that often moves in loud, chaotic rushes, you remain my calm sanctuary, a radiant light that brightens every corner of my thoughts.

Your name carries the sweetness of a blooming flower, and Tabassum—your captivating, effortless smile—has a unique magic all its own. It has the power to uplift the heaviest day and turn a simple afternoon into an unforgettable memory. Your warmth, grace, and kindness inspire me every single day.

Thank you for being my anchor, my confidante, and my greatest happiness. With you, conversations flow seamlessly into the night, and even quiet silences feel completely peaceful and complete. You bring out the absolute best in me just by being your genuine, beautiful self.

No matter where time leads us or how many tomorrows we share, please know this with certainty: my admiration, devotion, and love for you will remain steadfast and true.

Ever Devotedly Yours,

Md Hasan Al Mahmud
