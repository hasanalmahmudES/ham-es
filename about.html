Birth & Roots

Md Hasan Al Mahmud was born on 20 December 1999 in Katiadi, Kishoreganj, Bangladesh.

Growing up in the culturally vibrant region of Greater Mymensingh, his early environment fostered a profound appreciation for language, rigorous inquiry, and classical text study.

"Education is the deliberate bridge built between what one knows and what one has yet to impart to the world."
