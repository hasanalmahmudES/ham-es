Biography

Md Hasan Al Mahmud

Known to those close to him as Hasan and Al Mahmud, his path has run between two disciplines the scientific classroom and the study of Islamic scholarship before opening into a third: writing for learners of English.

Birth

Md Hasan Al Mahmud was born on 20 December 1999 in Katiadi, Kishoreganj. The date is held to less for its place on a birth certificate than for what it marks: the beginning point of an academic journey that would carry him from village madrasah classrooms to authorship.

Writing career

Alongside his formal studies, Md Hasan Al Mahmud turned to authorship, producing two resources aimed at learners working to master English grammar and pronunciation.

I.English Basic Grammar

Spoken & Written a foundational guide walking learners through the core structures of English as it is spoken and written.

II.The Oxford 3000 with IPA

A reference built around English's most essential vocabulary, paired with IPA transcriptions to guide accurate pronunciation.

In sum

Md Hasan Al Mahmud's path has been shaped by a steady pursuit of knowledge scientific, religious, and linguistic and by a wish to pass that knowledge on. His books stand as the clearest expression of that wish: education handed forward, one grammar rule and one word at a time.
