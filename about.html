Birth & Roots

Md Hasan Al Mahmud was born on 20 December 1999 in Katiadi, Kishoreganj, Bangladesh.

This date marks less a formal administrative record than the inception of a lifelong academic quest — taking him from the traditional village madrasah classrooms of Kishoreganj to authorship in English language instruction.

"Education is the deliberate bridge built between what one knows and what one has yet to bequeath to others."
